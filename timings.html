Before Your Batch

Arrive ready, swim happy

A few simple habits help every class start on time and keep the pool safe for everyone.

Arrive 10 minutes early

Time to change, shower and meet your coach on the deck.

Pack the essentials

Swimsuit, cap, goggles, towel and a water bottle.

Rinse before entering

A quick shower keeps the water clean and balanced.

Eat light

Have a small snack an hour before, not a heavy meal.

Good to Know

Timing questions, answered

Quick answers about changing slots, missed classes and holidays.

Can I change my batch later?

Yes. You can move to another batch once a month, subject to seat availability. Ask at the front desk or request it from your dashboard.

What happens if my child misses a class?

Missed classes can be made up in a matching batch within the same month. Please tell us at least a few hours in advance.

Is the pool open on public holidays?

The pool is closed on public holidays. We share the holiday list through the dashboard and our newsletter in advance.

How long is each session?

Every batch runs for one hour, including a short warm-up, skill practice and a safety check at the end.

What if my preferred batch is full?

Join the waitlist and we will contact you as soon as a seat opens. We can also suggest a nearby time with the same coach.

Can't find a time that works?

Tell us your preferred days and we will suggest the closest batch or arrange a private session.
