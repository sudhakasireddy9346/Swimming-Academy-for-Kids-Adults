Our Classic Programs

Find the batch that fits you

Three group programs for kids, teens and adults. Each one follows our safe, level-based curriculum.

Ages 4-8

Little Splashers

A gentle, playful first step into the water. Children build comfort, trust and basic swimming habits in a warm, shallow zone with a coach always within arm's reach.

Max 8 swimmers 45 min Levels 1-3

What your child will learn

  • Water comfort and breath control
  • Floating on front and back
  • Basic kicks and arm movement
  • Safe entry and exit skills
Enrol in Little Splashers
Ages 9-16

Stroke Masters

Technique-focused training for children and teens who can already swim. Structured drills and steady endurance work help them master all four competitive strokes.

Max 8 swimmers 60 min Levels 4-7

What they will learn

  • Freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly
  • Turns, starts and streamlines
  • Endurance and pacing
  • Water safety and rescue basics
Enrol in Stroke Masters
Adults

Adult Aqua Fit

Never too late to start. A supportive, judgement-free batch for adults who want low-impact fitness, or who want to overcome a fear of water and finally learn to swim.

Max 10 swimmers 50 min All levels

What you will get

  • Low-impact full-body workout
  • Beginner to advanced tracks
  • Fear-free, step-by-step coaching
  • Stamina, posture and flexibility gains
Enrol in Adult Aqua Fit

Good to Know

Be ready for your first class

A few simple things make every session smooth and safe.

What to bring

  • Swimsuit or trunks
  • Swim cap and goggles
  • Towel and change of clothes
  • Water bottle

What is included

  • Certified coach and lifeguard
  • Kickboards and training aids
  • Term progress report
  • Member dashboard access

Safety rules

  • Shower before entering the pool
  • Follow the coach at all times
  • No running on the pool deck
  • Inform us of any health condition

FAQ

Classic program questions

Quick answers to what swimmers and parents ask us most.

Do I need to know swimming before joining?

No. Little Splashers and Adult Aqua Fit welcome complete beginners. Stroke Masters is for swimmers who are already comfortable in the water.

What if my child misses a class?

Quarterly and half-yearly plans include makeup classes that can be used within the same term, subject to slot availability.

How do you decide the level?

A coach assesses water comfort, breathing and stroke ability during your free trial and recommends the right level.

Can I upgrade to Premium later?

Yes, you can switch to a Premium plan at any time and your level and progress carry over.
