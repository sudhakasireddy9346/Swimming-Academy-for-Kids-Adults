FAQ

Premium program questions

Quick answers before you book.

Who is Premium best for?

Swimmers who want faster progress, personal attention, flexible timings, or preparation for competitions.

Can I choose my coach?

You can request a preferred coach and we will match you based on availability and your goals.

How does rescheduling work?

Sessions can be rescheduled online from your dashboard, ideally at least 6 hours before the slot.

Can I move back to Classic?

Yes, you can switch between Classic and Premium at any time and your level and progress carry over.
