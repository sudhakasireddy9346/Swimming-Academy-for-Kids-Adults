FAQ

Quick answers before you ask

Is there a free trial class?

Yes. Every new swimmer can attend one free trial class so you can meet the coach and try the pool.

What should my child bring?

Swimwear, a towel, goggles, a swim cap and a water bottle. Lockers and showers are available.

Can adults with no swimming experience join?

Absolutely. Adult Aqua Fit starts from zero and moves at your pace with patient, certified coaches.

How do I pay fees and track attendance?

Log in to the member dashboard to enrol in batches, view attendance, check progress and pay online.

Prefer to talk? We are one call away.

Speak to our front desk for instant help with batches, fees and your free trial.
