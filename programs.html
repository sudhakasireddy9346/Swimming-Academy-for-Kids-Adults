FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers to what swimmers and parents ask us most.

Can I switch from Classic to Premium later?

Yes, you can switch at any time. Your current level and progress carry over to the new plan.

Do you offer a free trial?

Every new member can book one free trial class before enrolling in any programme.

Are lifeguards present in every session?

Yes. Certified lifeguards are on duty during all group batches and private sessions.

Can I pay my fees online?

Yes, fee payment, attendance and progress tracking are all available in the member dashboard.
